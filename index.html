Official Policy Statement

Setara QR — Privacy Policy & Terms

Application: Setara QR (Scanner & Generator) • Developer: Setara Manufacturing

Setara Manufacturing operates the mobile application Setara QR. This policy page outlines our rigorous standards regarding the privacy, data protection, and security protocols built into our utility tool.

Zero Data Tracking Principle: Setara QR does NOT transmit, harvest, log, sell, or rent your personal data, images, or scanned barcode contents to any external servers. All operations function strictly on-device.

1. Camera Permission Policy

Setara QR requests runtime access to your device's camera hardware solely for the following technical reasons:

  • Live real-time optical decoding of QR codes and barcodes via hardware viewfinder.
  • Enabling flash / torch toggle to allow code scanning in low-light environments.

We do not capture background photos, record live video streams, or upload camera buffers to third-party services.

2. Photo Gallery & File Storage Policy

When you opt to decode an existing image or export a created code:

  • Selected gallery images are analyzed directly inside local device memory using Google ML Kit and ZXing engine.
  • Generated QR codes and barcodes are exported locally to your device's public Pictures/SetaraQR storage via standard Android MediaStore API without external network requests.

3. Third-Party Frameworks & Monetization

Setara QR integrates compliant, industry-grade libraries strictly adhering to Google Play policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays banner advertisements to fund software maintenance. AdMob may utilize standard Google Advertising Identifiers (AAID) for ad fraud prevention and analytics as governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google ML Kit Vision: Runs on-device machine learning models for low-latency barcode identification without requiring an internet connection.

4. User Data Retention & Bulk Deletion

Setara QR places data ownership fully in the hands of the user:

  • Both scanned and generated records are saved in local private sandbox storage (Encrypted SharedPreferences).
  • Users can select individual or multiple records for immediate batch deletion or sharing.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately and irreversibly destroys all local history logs.

5. Children's Privacy Compliance (COPPA / GDPR)

Setara QR is designed as a neutral utility application suitable for general audiences. We do not deliberately solicit, collect, or store information from children under the age of 13.

6. Terms of Service & Usage Disclaimer

Setara QR is distributed as a free utility. Users must exercise reasonable caution before browsing websites, dialing numbers, or connecting to Wi-Fi networks decoded from third-party physical barcodes. Setara Manufacturing bears no liability for external actions initiated outside the app.
