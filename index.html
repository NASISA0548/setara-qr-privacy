Setara QR — Privacy Policy & Terms

Application: Setara QR (Scanner & Generator) • Last Updated: September 2026

Setara Manufacturing operates the mobile application Setara QR. This policy page outlines our policies regarding the collection, handling, and security of user data when using our utility tool.

Core Privacy Principle: Setara QR does NOT transmit, harvest, sell, or rent your personal information, scanned contents, or device images to any remote servers. All processing operates strictly on-device.

1. Camera Permission Policy

Setara QR requires runtime access to your device's camera strictly for:

  • Decoding barcodes and QR codes in real-time via hardware video feed.
  • Enabling flash control to illuminate dark scanning environments.

We do NOT record video feeds, take silent photographs, or transmit camera capture buffers to external parties.

2. Storage & Photo Gallery Access

When selecting images from your device gallery to decode a barcode or when saving generated barcodes:

  • Images are decoded on the local device using Google ML Kit and ZXing engine.
  • No bitmap data or personal galleries leave the client runtime environment.
  • Saved codes are written directly to your public Pictures directory using standard Android MediaStore protocols.

3. Third-Party Frameworks & Advertising

To support ongoing engineering and provide free services, Setara QR integrates reputable industry-standard SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver non-personalized or personalized banner advertisements conforming to Google Play Developer Policies. AdMob may utilize standard hardware identifiers (AAID) for fraud protection and basic ad operations.
  • Google ML Kit Vision: Operates entirely on-device to deliver low-latency barcode identification without internet dependency.

4. User Data Retention & Deletion

Setara QR provides localized History management:

  • All scan and generation records are held strictly in private local device storage (Android Encrypted Shared Preferences).
  • Users hold absolute ownership and can clear individual items or perform complete batch purges at any time via the History dashboard.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately purges all localized application records.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Setara QR does not solicit, target, or knowingly store personal data from children under the age of 13. The application is built as a general utility tool compliant with Google Play Families and Child Safety requirements.

6. Terms of Service

By using Setara QR, you agree to inspect and exercise due caution before navigating to third-party web domains, sending messages, or initiating Wi-Fi handshakes decoded from physical codes. Setara Manufacturing bears no liability for external hyperlinks created by third-party barcode publishers.
