Setara QR - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to Setara QR. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how our application handles your information.

1. Camera & Gallery Permissions

Setara QR requires camera access exclusively to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. We do not record, store, or transmit your camera feed. Images picked from your gallery are processed locally on your device solely for decoding purposes.

2. Data Collection & Processing

All barcode scanning and generation operations are performed 100% locally on your device. We do not operate external servers to collect, upload, or sell your personal data or generated codes.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

Our app utilizes Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google ML Kit for on-device barcode recognition. These services may collect device identifiers and technical analytics in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy

4. Terms & Conditions

Setara QR is provided free of charge as a productivity tool. You are responsible for inspecting and verifying third-party URLs or actions embedded within scanned codes before navigating to them.
